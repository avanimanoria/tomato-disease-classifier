## 2. Metric definitions (code references)
- `targetDepositionPct` (primary) = 100 x spray mass on canopy discs of truly diseased plants / emitted spray (`sprayMetrics.m`; `plantDose.m`, canopy radius 0.2 m).
- `treatmentSuccessPct` = 100 x truly diseased plants whose canopy received >= 30% of one nominal dose / all truly diseased plants (`sprayMetrics.m`, `treatedDoseFraction`). Threshold-based and assumption-sensitive.
- `offTargetPct` = 100 - targetDepositionPct (healthy canopies + other ground + unresolved); `offFieldPct` = 100 x deposited mass outside the field polygon / emitted (`advancedMetrics.m`).
- Why dashboard values differ: Experiment E (55% deposition, Fully Corrected) uses default physics; Experiment F adds payload, dynamics, atmosphere, actuation, gusts and estimators in every run, so its means (about 27% at best) are not comparable to E.
- Mass balance: emitted = deposited + evaporated + unresolved (kernel tail beyond 4 sigma / outside grid); verified >= 0 and < 1% in `tests/MassEnergySprayConservationTest`.

## 4. Hypotheses
**H1 rank reversal - observed as reported (3 pairs, tolerance 1 pp on the single train field).** Margins and replication:

| Config A | Config B | A better (conditions) | B better | max margin (pp) | reversal also on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PID / wind compensation | PurePursuit / wind + lag comp. | 2 | 7 | 7.69 | 0 of 2 test fields |
| PID / wind + lag compensation | PurePursuit / wind compensation | 5 | 3 | 4.07 | 2 of 2 test fields |
| PurePursuit / wind compensation | PurePursuit / wind + lag comp. | 1 | 4 | 6.77 | 0 of 2 test fields |
Interpretation: reversals between near-equivalent configurations (same compensation family) with margins of a few pp on one field; treat as modelled sensitivity, not established fact. Rank reversal does not imply a selector gain.

**H2 selector beats best fixed - NOT SUPPORTED (preserved).** fixed 26.95%, selector 26.60%, oracle 27.04%, gain -0.35 pp [-0.63, -0.13]. Investigation: oracle head-room over best fixed is only 0.09 pp; the selector switched away from the best fixed configuration in 19 of 24 conditions, based on single-field train gaps (median top-2 gap 0.01 pp; in 12 of 24 conditions the top two configurations tie EXACTLY because wind and wind + lag compensation are identical when the valve delay is 0, so the choice there is the first in list order - an artifact) that are smaller than field-to-field variation; the six configurations are two near-tied families (spread within compensated configs 1.12 pp, within uncompensated 0.06 pp). Conclusion: with these options there is almost nothing to select between, so a per-condition selector overfits. No retuning on test was done.

**H4 robustness - kernel-specific only.** Spearman 1.00 between configuration rankings with the circular and the (uncalibrated) wind-stretched kernel; same best fixed: YES. It does NOT establish cross-crop or cross-hardware robustness (no sourced profiles were tested).

## 5. Valve delay and lag compensation
Spray release in this engine happens while HOVERING (settle 1 s, then a 2 s dwell). A 0.5 s valve delay therefore only shifts the last 25% of each dose into the departure. Wind-compensated configurations: deposition 25.54% (no delay) vs 23.42% (0.5 s delay, mean displacement 0.08 m); with lag compensation 25.54% (displacement 0.00 m). The effect is small because the drone is stationary for most of the spray. In a MOVING release at 1.5 m/s the same delay would displace the spray by 0.75 m - moving release is not modelled (engine sprays only at hover); it is listed as an open item, not silently added.

## 6. Limitations
- One train field, two test fields, one replicate per cell: confidence intervals understate field-level uncertainty.
- Spray kernels, gust model, estimator noise and physics parameters are uncalibrated assumptions.
- The scenario-conditioned selector is the simplest possible (lookup of the best train configuration per condition).
- Experiment F stays frozen; any corrected or extended study must be a new versioned run (Experiment F2).
